MATH/CCM 472/572 · Solving Linear Systems

Solving Linear Systems: interactive explorers

Each page goes with a section of the course notes. Nothing needs installing; every number is computed live in your browser in the same double-precision arithmetic NumPy uses. All pages work with a keyboard and a screen reader.

  1. Why linear systems?
    Drag data points and watch curve fitting become Ax = b. See why the same A keeps coming back with new b’s.
  2. LU, step by step
    Step through elimination as the multipliers fill in L, then solve several systems with one factorization. Includes a predict mode and optional pivoting.
  3. What LU costs, and why not the inverse
    Where ⅔n³ comes from, run-time predictions, and the inverse losing on cost, accuracy and memory.
  4. Why pivoting?
    Watch a tiny pivot wipe out an answer in floating point, and one row swap fix it.
  5. Norms and condition numbers
    Norms as shapes, matrix norms as stretches, and two nearly parallel lines that amplify a tiny nudge.
  6. Least squares, the normal equations, and QR
    Residuals drawn as real squares, the projection picture, a step-through of the uniqueness proof, normal equations vs. QR, and Gram–Schmidt in 3D.
