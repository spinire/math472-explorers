What a matrix does to a circle

Every matrix turns the unit circle into an ellipse. Two perpendicular input directions land on the ellipse’s axes: Av1 = σ1u1 and Av2 = σ2u2. Drag x around the circle, then step through split → stretch → add to watch A = UΣVT happen.

Things to try

  • Rotation. The circle stays a circle, so every direction is stretched by 1 and σ1 = σ2 = 1. Which directions are v1, v2 now?
  • Rank one. The ellipse collapses to a segment, σ2 = 0, and cond(A) = ∞. What does A do to v2?
  • Nearly singular. A long, thin ellipse. Compare cond(A) with the ratio of its axes.
  • Flips orientation. The colors run the other way around the ellipse. The “add” stage now includes a reflection.
  • Best rank-one approximation. Turn it on and drag x. Where is the error largest, and how large does it get?
